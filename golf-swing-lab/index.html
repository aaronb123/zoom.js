Your video stays on your phone. Only scores and a few small stills are saved.

Film it like this

  • Whole body and club in shot, about 3–4 m away, phone at hip height.
  • Normal video at 60 fps is best (iPhone: Settings › Camera › Record Video › 1080p at 60). Skip the Slo-mo mode: it speeds up and slows down, which throws off the timing.
  • Only you in the frame. At Topgolf, film from the side of your bay.
  • Hold your finish for a couple of seconds.
