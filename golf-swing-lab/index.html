Film it so the numbers mean something

  1. Pick one angle. Face-on: phone straight in front of your chest. Down the line: behind your hands, pointing at the target.
  2. Whole body plus club in frame, about 3–4 m away, phone at hip-to-chest height on a stand or bag.
  3. Landscape or portrait both work. Use 60 fps if your camera has it. If you shoot slo-mo, set “Recorded at” to match.
  4. One person in shot. At Topgolf, film from the side of the bay so the next bay is out of frame.
  5. Hold your finish for a couple of seconds. The lab scores balance too.
